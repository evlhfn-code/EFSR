# Items, Diamonds and the Dealer

## Diamonds

- **Diamond Rain** comes every ~15 minutes. In the original game it was 10 drops x 15 = 150 diamonds per rain (about 600 an hour). With the modded 10x that's about **1,500 per rain and 6,000 an hour**, if the boost applies to rain. Check this in game.
- **VIP** gives 2,500 diamonds and an Epic Box every day. The **Starter Pack** gives 5,000. The **Pumpkin Boss quest** gives 2,500.
- You can also earn diamonds from achievements and by selling items.
- **Cap: 1,000,000 diamonds** (update #8). Don't sit near the cap; spend first.

## Best uses

1. **Deviled Egg** (+999% White Eggs for 60 min, once a day) and **Hasty Hen** (1 chicken per level for 60 min, 10,000 diamonds, every 6h). Time them together.
2. **Trophy Boxes** (1,000). Each trophy gives its Hero +250% damage, plus 10% per Wonderland level. They stack and they trade.
3. **Mythic Boxes** (7,500) until you have three Mythic weapons covering all nine abilities, and Mythic wings.
4. **Auto-Eggs** (4,000, then +2,000 each). Put them on your best Hero (auto-train) or on abilities.
5. **EGG9000** and **Timelapses** for the long pushes.

|---|---|---|---|

| Epic | 40% | +4 | 6% |
| Legendary | 50% | +5 | 8% |
| Mythic | 65% | +6 | no Mythic trails |

Don't open Mythic Boxes looking for a trail; Mythic trails don't exist. Modded weapons can also roll **traits**.

## Trophies and Reforge

- One trophy per Hero is the standard. About 10 copies of one trophy are needed for another 10x on that Hero.
- **Reforge** a trophy for 200 diamonds (random, from Heroes you've hired) or 8,000 (pick one).
- Trophy Boxes are also the common trade currency, for example payment for idle pushing.

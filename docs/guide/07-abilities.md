# Abilities and Combos

Abilities come from Hero upgrades and are lost when you Retire or Rebirth. Hotkeys are 1 to 9.

| Key | Ability | From | Effect | Base duration | Base cooldown | Duration egg |

| 9 | Hamburger | Gordon Ramsay Lv 100 | Removes 60 min from the last ability's cooldown | - | 60 min | - |

Each duration egg adds **+2 s per level**.

## Weapons cut cooldowns

A weapon cuts the cooldown of **3 random abilities**: Common 10%, Uncommon 20%, Rare 30%, Epic 40%, Legendary 50%, Mythic 65%. The goal is three Mythic weapons covering all nine abilities. A Mythic weapon on Decoy and Hamburger turns their 60 min into 21 min.

## Combos

- **Boss killer:** Decoy > Nine-Tails > Super Saiyan > Battle Shout, then attack the boss yourself.
- **Double Decoy** (works again in modded): Decoy > Hamburger > Nine-Tails > Decoy > Fertilizer. Hamburger doesn't use up Decoy, so both abilities get doubled.
- **Egg burst on an Eggception:** Decoy > Hamburger > Fertilizer > Decoy > Treasure Hunt > Tornado (8 > 9 > 5 > 8 > 4 > 1).
- **Always on:** with every duration egg at about level 3,585 (2-hour durations) and a Mythic weapon on Decoy and Hamburger, cycle Decoy > Hamburger > X > Decoy > Y every 21 minutes to keep everything Decoyed.

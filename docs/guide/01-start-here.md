# Start Here

Egg Farm Simulator [MODDED] is SirMing's own re-creation of the 2018 version of Egg Farm Simulator, run by the EFSR group, with four permanent multipliers:

| Boost | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| White Eggs | **250,000x** |
| Diamonds | **10x** |
| Black Eggs | **500x** |
| Gold Eggs | **25x** |

The sibling game, Egg Farm Simulator [REBORN], is the same re-creation without those boosts. Reborn, Modded and Sandbox all update together (patch #7).

## The loop

You kill (or "feed") chickens on your farm plot for **White Eggs**, spend them on **Heroes** (the game also calls them Farmers) and push to higher **farm levels**. Every reset layer trades your progress for a stronger currency:

| Layer | Unlocks at | You give up | You get | Spend it on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Retire** | Queen Elizabeth II at Lv 150 | White Eggs, Heroes, farm level | Pending **Black Eggs** | [Easter Eggs](04-easter-eggs.md), or keep them unspent (+10% DPS each) |
| **Rebirth** | Beat farm level 300 | All of the above plus Black Eggs and Easter Eggs | Pending **Gold Eggs** and Rebirth Power | [Phoenix Eggs](05-phoenix-eggs.md) |
| **Ascend** | Farm level 300,000 | All of the above plus Gold Eggs | Pending **Platinum Eggs** | [Platinum upgrades](06-platinum-eggs.md) |

Your inventory (weapons, wings, trails, trophies) and diamonds are never reset.

## Where Black Eggs come from

- **Mutated Bosses.** Every fifth level is a boss. From level 100 on, each boss has a chance to spawn Mutated the first time you reach it in a run. Levels 100, 110, 130 and 200 are always Mutated. This is the main source.
- **Hero levels.** Every 2,000 Hero levels you buy in a run adds pending Black Eggs.

Black Eggs only become yours when you Retire. Lifetime Black Eggs then set how many Gold Eggs you can claim. In the modded game, Retire Black Eggs count toward Gold Eggs (update #8).

## Your first hour

1. Claim a plot, hire Master Pai Mei and Tarzan, and keep buying the newest Hero you can afford. At 250,000x eggs the early Heroes are almost free.
2. Use **Buy Max** on Heroes. Every 25 levels after 175 gives a Hero 4x damage (5x from 525 to 725), and every 1,000 levels gives 10x.
3. Push to Queen Elizabeth II level 150, then keep going for Mutated bosses before your first Retire.
4. Collect every [Diamond Rain](08-items-and-diamonds.md) (every ~15 min) and ride the [Egg Rocket](09-bosses-and-events.md) for free boxes.

# Progression by Stage

## Stage 1: Fresh start to the first Retire

- Hire every new Hero as soon as you can afford it. Use Buy Max on your newest Hero, then spread levels back down the list. Old Heroes catch up fast because of the 4x every 25 levels.
- Queen Elizabeth II at **level 150** unlocks Retirement. Don't Retire the moment you can. Push past level 100 first so the guaranteed Mutated bosses (100, 110, 130) pay out.
- Unspent Black Eggs give **+10% DPS each**. Good Knight gives **+11% per Black Egg**, so once Good Knight is hatched it beats holding Black Eggs. Everything else waits until you have a pile.
- The wiki rule of thumb was 10 to 20 Black Eggs for the first Retire and about 100 before the first Rebirth. With 500x Black Eggs you will blow past that. Use the [Run calculator](../../index.html#calc-run) to see what your next Retire is worth.

## Stage 2: Retire loops and the push to 300

- Each Retire, hatch Easter Eggs in this order of value: **Good Knight, Dragon, Demeaning, Faberge**, then Newton. See [Easter Eggs](04-easter-eggs.md).
- Pick up the ability Heroes: Naruto (Nine-Tails), Jack Sparrow (Treasure Hunt), Old MacDonald (Fertilizer), Goku (Super Saiyan), Loki (Decoy) and Gordon Ramsay (Hamburger). They make bosses and Eggceptions much easier.
- When a boss walls you, chain **Decoy > Nine-Tails > Super Saiyan > Battle Shout** and attack it yourself.
- Beat farm level **300** and Rebirth right away. Don't Retire after 300, or you lose the run.

## Stage 3: After the first Rebirth

- Gold Eggs buy [Phoenix Eggs](05-phoenix-eggs.md). Start with **Earth and Water together** (Earth multiplies Black Eggs by 10 x Earth² + 1; Water makes every Easter Egg cheaper), then **Fire** for raw damage.
- **Rebirth Power** grows with lifetime Gold Eggs claimed. It multiplies Black Eggs by (1 + RP) for every boss level past 100, so it dominates later. The modded cap is 30% (31.2% with upgrades).
- Speed comes from fewer chickens per level: **Idol** (Easter) plus **Sound** (Phoenix) plus **Resonance** (Platinum), down to the floor of 2. **Hasty Hen** sets 1 for an hour.
- Keep the Mutated chance at 100%: **Radioactive** (Easter) plus **Magnetism** (Phoenix). The chance falls 2% every 500 levels, so it needs topping up as you go deeper.
- Keep boss timers alive: **Hourglass** plus **Wind**. Keep boss health down: **King Cobra** plus **Acid**.

## Stage 4: The 300,000 push and Ascension

- Ascension needs farm level **300,000**. Use EGG9000 (auto-progress), Timelapses, Egg Beater (now 24h) and AFK auto-rejoin to grind it out.
- At about 50 levels a minute (2 chickens per level, one-shotting), 300,000 levels is roughly 100 hours of clean play. Level Skip (Platinum) and Hasty Hen cut that a lot.
- Ascend and spend Platinum Eggs with the [Platinum planner](06-platinum-eggs.md).

## Stage 5: Endgame

- Push Rebirth Power to the cap with Rebirth Cap Increase and Rebirth Boost.
- Abundance (+1% Eggs, Black Eggs and Gold Eggs per level) is the uncapped sink.
- Idle push with friends (see [Bosses and Events](09-bosses-and-events.md#idle-pushing)), and stack trophies with Wonderland.

## Fast and slow zones

Players describe a run as alternating fast and slow zones. It's fast right after a Rebirth, after hiring Queen Elsa, and between about 5% and 20% Rebirth Power. It slows down before the first Rebirth, from Antman through Annie and Tibbers until Deadpool and Michael Jackson, and once Black Egg growth levels off near the Rebirth Power cap. In a slow zone, more Retires and Idol/Sound levels help more than Timelapses.

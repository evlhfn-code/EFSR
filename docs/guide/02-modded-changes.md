# What's Different in Modded

Everything here comes from the EFSR [REBORN] Discord update and patch logs (#2 to #8, the bug-fix update and the hotfix). The base game rules come from the original game's wiki. Where the two disagree, the Discord wins.

## Permanent boosts

| | Original | Modded |
|---|---|---|
| White Eggs | 1x | 250,000x |
| Diamonds | 1x | 10x |
| Black Eggs | 1x | 500x |
| Gold Eggs | 1x | 25x |

Events stack on top. The 100K-visits event (patch #7) added 40x Eggs and 2x Damage.

## New systems

- **Platinum Eggs and Ascension** (update #3). This is a third reset layer above Rebirth. You Ascend at farm level **300,000** (patch #7; it was 50,000). Pending Gold Eggs count toward Platinum Eggs, and pending Platinum Eggs show in the egg bar (update #8). See [Platinum Eggs](06-platinum-eggs.md).
- **Hasty Hen** (Dealer, update #8). 1 chicken per level for 60 minutes. Costs 10,000 diamonds, once every 6 hours.
- **Upgrade Best Easter Eggs** button (update #3, reworked in patch #7 and "worth using now").
- **Collectable Chicken Leg** (update #3).
- **F button** at level 1,000+ jumps you to Level 1 or your Best Level (update #8).
- **Skip Retire** (Robux or diamonds). It banks your pending reward and makes you eligible to Rebirth without resetting you (bug-fix update).
- **Weapon traits.** Modded weapons can roll traits. Patch #6 fixed duplicate traits losing their ability stats.
- **Halloween event.** A Pumpkin Boss replaces the Alien for the event, with Halloween quests. See [Bosses and Events](09-bosses-and-events.md).

## Quality of life

- AFK freely: the game auto-rejoins you just before the idle kick, and rejoining gives your plot and spot back.
- After a server restart your combo is kept, you reclaim a farm and return to your position, and ability cooldowns reset.
- Auto abilities fire as soon as they're ready.
- Settings: Skip Hero Walk, Hide other Heroes, Pet Chick toggle.
- Hero levels show in short numbers (1,000K). Leaderboards show Ascensions.

# Bosses, Events and Pushing

## Boss levels

Every 5th farm level is a boss with **10x** a normal chicken's health and a **30 s** timer. If the timer runs out, the boss heals to full. Every 500 levels the game gets harder:

| Every 500 levels | Change | Counter |
|---|---|---|
| Boss health multiplier | +0.4x | King Cobra (up to -5x) and Acid (-2.5x each); floor 5x |
| Boss timer | -2 s | Hourglass (up to +30 s) and Wind (+22.5 s each); floor 2 s |
| Mutated chance | -2% (from a base of 25%) | Radioactive (up to +75%) and Magnetism (+18.75% each); floor 5%, max 100% |
| Chickens per level | +0.1 (from a base of 10) | Idol (up to -8), Sound (about -1 each), Resonance; floor 2 |
| Eggception chance | x0.994 (from a base of 1%) | Scientist (up to 100x) and Electricity (+100x each) |

The [Level calculator](../../index.html#calc-level) shows all of these for any level and your egg levels, and where each one runs out.

## Mutated Bosses

- They start at level 100. Levels 100, 110, 130 and 200 are always Mutated, and the wiki's chicken table also marks every 100th level from 300 to 800 as always Mutated.
- A boss can only be Mutated the first time you reach it in a run. Retire or Rebirth to reset.
- Timelapse-skipped bosses never drop Black Eggs. In modded, Timeskip kills each boss once.
- Update #8 fixed a bug where every boss on a level was Mutated.

## Eggceptions

Eggceptions are chicken-sized golden eggs worth 10x a normal chicken. Newton adds +50% per level, and Fertilizer plus Eggplant turn them into huge White Egg spikes. During Halloween they become pumpkins.

## Egg Rocket and the Alien Chicken Boss

- The rocket launches every ~15 minutes from the middle of the map. Stand in the purple circle.
- All your upgrades are off on the moon: hits deal 1 (crits 5). The boss has about 800 HP solo and more with more players. You have 5 minutes.
- Rewards: Common Box for 18.75% damage, Uncommon for 50%, Rare for the kill.
- Take Auto-Eggs out of the egg cannon and don't have Tornado active before you go up. Both cut your damage to the boss.

## Halloween: Pumpkin Boss

- Update #2 added a Halloween-themed Alien Boss ("insane rewards") and Halloween quests.
- Patch #7 cut the Pumpkin Boss's health by 1.3x and added 2 s to its attack interval. The bug-fix update fixed the Rolling Pumpkin hitting from out of range.
- Quest rewards: 5 Gold Eggs (go to pending) and 2,500 diamonds for the Pumpkin Boss quest (update #8).

## Idle pushing

A stronger player attacks your chickens while you stay idle. The **AFK** Easter Egg (+25% DPS per level while idle) powers up their hits on your farm.

- **Modded timing:** idle starts after **15 s** (was 60 s) and combo lasts **10 min** (was 4).
- Refresh your combo (the Turtle egg's +0.01% DPS per attack per level) with one click before it runs out, then go idle again.
- Pet Chick and Auto-Eggs in the cannon break idle. Put Auto-Eggs on Hero training instead.
- Push speed caps around 50 levels per minute at 2 chickens per level when one-shotting.
- Pushers usually charge in Trophy Boxes (1 to 3 per hour).
